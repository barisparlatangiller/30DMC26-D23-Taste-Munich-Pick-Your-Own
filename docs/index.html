Pick your own Munich

Method and limits

Fields. Pick-your-own fields, flower fields and pumpkin stands named by the farms and by Munich city guides for the 2026 season (Hofis/Hofreiter, Erdbeer-Lang, Familie Wolf, Storz, Eberle, Beerengarten Rothschwaige, Morigl Hof), plus the city's seven Krautgärten (vegetable plots you rent for a season and harvest yourself). Locations were geocoded from the addresses and street corners the sources give: a dot marks the address, the corner, the street or, where only a village is named, the village.

OpenStreetMap. Not one feature in the region carries the tag pick_your_own=yes. What OSM knows sits in names: "Blumen selber schneiden", "Erdbeerfeld zum Selberpflücken". Those are added when no researched field of the same kind is within 400 m. Farm shops (shop=farm) are shown as context.

Seasons. From the sources where given; otherwise typical Bavarian seasons: strawberries May–July, raspberries, blueberries and currants July–September, cherries June–July, apples September–October, flowers April–October, pumpkins September–October, Krautgärten May–mid-November.

Check before you go. Strawberry fields move every year (crop rotation) and open only when the fruit is ripe. Farms post the open fields each day on their websites.
